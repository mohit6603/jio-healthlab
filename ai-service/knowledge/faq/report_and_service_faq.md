# FAQ: Reports, Delivery and Services

## How do I check the status of my report?

Every request is tracked on the HealthLab dashboard from registration through to
delivery. Search by your report number, your name, or filter by test type,
branch or city.

## What does "urgent" priority mean?

An urgent request is one the requesting clinician has marked as clinically
time-sensitive. Urgent samples move to the front of the laboratory queue and
can trigger an unscheduled courier run rather than waiting for the next
scheduled one. Urgent priority changes scheduling; it does not change how the
test itself is performed.

## Why is my report taking longer than expected?

Common operational reasons include:

- the workstation queue was deep when your sample arrived;
- a high volume of urgent samples was being processed ahead of routine work;
- an analyser was down for maintenance or calibration;
- your sample had to be re-collected;
- your sample was collected shortly after a scheduled courier run departed;
- results are held in review pending verification.

## Can I add a test to a sample I have already given?

Sometimes. Whether an add-on is possible depends on the tube type collected,
the volume remaining, how the sample was stored and how long ago it was taken.
Contact your branch with your report number and the laboratory will confirm.

## Which branches are available?

JIO HealthLab operates collection centres across several cities, each linked to
a central processing laboratory. Branch and city are recorded on every request
and shown on the dashboard.

## Are my details kept private?

Patient identifiers are stored in the laboratory information system with
access limited to authorised roles. The AI assistant in this application works
only from the laboratory's published knowledge documents and from de-identified
report metadata — it is not given patient names, contact details or addresses.

## Can the AI assistant tell me what my results mean?

No. The assistant can explain what a test measures, what the terminology means,
and how the laboratory workflow operates, using the knowledge documents it has
been given. It does not diagnose, does not interpret your individual results
clinically, and does not recommend treatment. Anything it produces is
informational only and should be discussed with a qualified clinician.

## What if my question is not covered here?

Contact your collection branch with your report number. If the assistant does
not have relevant information in its knowledge base, it will say so rather than
guess.
