# SOP: Sample Collection and Labelling

## Purpose

To define how specimens are collected, labelled and verified at JIO HealthLab
collection points so that every sample reaching the laboratory is traceable to
exactly one patient and one request.

## Scope

Applies to all phlebotomy staff and collection-centre technicians across every
branch.

## Patient identification

1. Ask the patient to state their full name and date of birth. Do not read the
   details out for confirmation — open questions prevent mis-identification.
2. Match the stated details against the request form and the booking record.
3. Where the patient cannot respond, confirm identity with an accompanying
   person or the ward record.
4. Do not proceed if any detail disagrees. Escalate to the branch supervisor.

## Order of draw

When multiple tubes are collected from a single venepuncture, fill them in the
standard order of draw to prevent additive carry-over between tubes:

1. Blood culture bottles
2. Coagulation tube (citrate)
3. Serum tube, with or without gel
4. Heparin tube
5. EDTA tube
6. Fluoride-oxalate (glycolysis inhibitor) tube

## Labelling

Label every tube **at the patient's side**, immediately after collection and
before leaving the patient. Pre-labelling tubes before collection and batch
labelling afterwards are both prohibited.

Each label must carry the patient identifier, the accession number, the
collection date and time, and the collector's initials.

## Mixing and volume

Additive tubes must be inverted gently the number of times specified for that
tube type. Under-filled additive tubes change the ratio of blood to additive
and are rejected.

## Rejection criteria

A sample is rejected and re-collection requested when it is:

- unlabelled, mislabelled, or labelled with details that disagree with the
  request form;
- clotted, when the test requires an unclotted sample;
- haemolysed, for analytes affected by haemolysis;
- under-filled or over-filled in an additive tube;
- collected into the wrong tube type;
- outside its permitted transport window or temperature range.

The requesting location is notified of every rejection with the reason recorded
against the accession number.

## Record keeping

Every collection is recorded against its accession number with the collector's
identity and timestamp. Rejections and re-collections are logged so that
recurring causes can be reviewed at branch level.
